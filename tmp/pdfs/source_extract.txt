DaGoal is a gamified daily activity planner developed to encourage users to reduce
excessive screen time by participating in productive real-world activities. The application
generates personalized daily quests based on user preferences, including physical, mental,
and creative activities. It also applies gamification through experience points, gold
currency, levels, streaks, achievements, avatar customization, and cosmetic rewards to
encourage continued participation. Selected timed quests may also restrict distracting
applications to help users remain focused on their activities. DaGoal
The system was developed as an Android application that supports both online and offline
use. It provides different methods of task verification, including manual confirmation,
repetition-based progress, timed activities, and step tracking. Firebase supports account
authentication and online synchronization, while local storage allows important features to
remain available without an internet connection. DaGoal
The application underwent manual functional testing involving 97 test cases, all of which
received a Passed status under the conditions used during testing. The developed system
was also evaluated by 57 respondents after a three-day testing period. The software quality
evaluation produced a grand mean of 3.31 with a standard deviation of 0.30, interpreted as
Strongly Agree. The respondents gave positive evaluations in terms of usability, enjoyment
and engagement, screen time management, performance, and overall experience.
Overall, the findings indicate that DaGoal successfully implemented its intended functions
and was generally accepted by respondents as an engaging application that may support
better screen time awareness and encourage participation in productive daily activities
.

Project Context
People spend excessive hours looking at digital screens both globally and locally.
A report by DataReportal (2023) states that internet users worldwide spend more
than six hours daily online, while users in the Philippines average more than nine
hours per day, making screen consumption a significant lifestyle concern. At the
same time, the World Health Organization (n.d.) emphasizes that physical inactivity
and sedentary behavior remain major contributors to health risks and reduced
overall well-being. These conditions may lead to reduced physical movement,
lower productivity, and limited engagement in hobbies.
The proponents selected this research problem based on observed experiences
among students and young adults who frequently engage in prolonged screen use
for social media, entertainment, gaming, and academic tasks. To further validate
these observations, the researchers conducted a survey among selected respondents
and determined their daily screen time habits, lifestyle routines, and level of interest
in a gamified activity planner.
This project will investigate how gamified daily planning may help reduce
excessive screen time and promote healthier routines. Specifically, it seeks to
determine what types of physical, mental, and creative activities may effectively
replace passive screen consumption, how virtual rewards influence user
consistency, and what level of scheduling flexibility encourages long-term use.
Gamification serves as the central behavioral framework of the proposed system.
Previous studies suggest that reward systems, virtual progression, and avatar
customization may improve user engagement and long-term retention in wellness
applications. These mechanics may motivate users to consistently complete real-
world activities by providing experience points, virtual currency, streak
progression, and cosmetic rewards.
To address these concerns, the proponents propose a mobile application called
DaGoal, a gamified daily activity planner designed to help users replace passive
screen time with productive hobbies. The application generates personalized daily
STI College Pasay-EDSA 1\n\n--- PAGE 11 ---\nquests based on user preferences collected through an initial questionnaire and
applies controlled randomization to maintain task variety. These quests include
physical activities such as walking and exercise, mental activities such as reading
and journaling, and creative hobbies such as drawing and writing.
The system is designed to support both online and offline use, allowing users to
access their quests, progress records, rewards, and avatar customization features
even without an internet connection. When internet access is available, the system
can use features that require online connectivity. In addition, selected quests may
include a social media blocking feature that temporarily restricts access to user-
selected applications for a specific period to further support screen time reduction.
To support the application’s app-blocking feature, DaGoal will use the App Usage
Stats API together with Usage Access Permission to detect when selected
distracting applications are opened. Accessibility Services may also be used to
improve foreground app detection and allow the system to interrupt or restrict
access during active timed quests. Through these Android capabilities, DaGoal can
support controlled app blocking while still following the device’s security and
permission requirements.
The significance of this study lies in its potential to provide users with a practical
and engaging method for building healthier habits, improving productivity, and
reducing sedentary digital routines. Through the integration of gamification and
structured activity planning, DaGoal aims to offer a meaningful alternative to
excessive screen consumption among students and young adults.
STI College Pasay-EDSA 2\n\n--- PAGE 12 ---\nPurpose and Description
DaGoal is a gamified daily activity planner and wellness mobile application
designed to help users reduce excessive screen time by encouraging them to
spend more time on useful real-world activities. The project aims to give users
a simple and engaging way to improve their daily routines by suggesting
activities that can support productivity, physical activity, creativity, and
healthier use of digital devices.
One major function of DaGoal is its daily quest system. When users first use
the application, they answer a short questionnaire about their interests, preferred
activities, and daily habits. Based on these preferences, the application provides
daily quests that may include physical activities such as walking and exercising,
mental activities such as reading and journaling, and creative activities such as
drawing and writing. This helps users receive activities that are more suitable to
their interests instead of being given the same tasks every day.
Another major function is the flexible task management system. Users are
allowed to skip or replace selected quests when an activity does not match their
schedule, situation, or available time. This gives users more control over their
daily activities and prevents the application from becoming too strict or difficult
to follow. Through this feature, users can continue using the application even
when their daily routines change.
DaGoal also includes a screen time control activity for selected quests. Some
activities may challenge users to avoid chosen social media or distracting
applications for a specific period of time. This feature helps users focus on the
activity they are completing instead of immediately returning to scrolling,
gaming, or other digital distractions. It supports the main purpose of the project
by encouraging users to spend more time away from unnecessary screen use.
STI College Pasay-EDSA 3\n\n--- PAGE 13 ---\nAnother important function is the progress tracking system. The application
keeps track of completed quests, daily progress, streaks, and other
accomplishments. This allows users to see how consistently they are completing
their activities and how their progress develops over time. Seeing their progress
may also encourage users to continue following healthier and more productive
routines.
The application also uses a reward and customization system to make daily
activities more enjoyable. Users can earn experience points, gold,
achievements, and other rewards after completing quests. The earned gold may
be used to purchase cosmetic items and customize the user's in-app avatar.
These rewards give users additional motivation to complete their daily activities
and maintain their progress.
What makes DaGoal unique is that it does not only monitor how much time
users spend on their devices. Instead, it provides users with specific real-world
activities that they can do as alternatives to unnecessary screen use. By
combining personalized daily quests, flexible task choices, screen time control
activities, progress tracking, rewards, and avatar customization, DaGoal
provides a more active and engaging approach to digital wellness. The project
is relevant to students and young adults who regularly spend long periods using
digital devices and want a practical way to build healthier and more productive
daily routines.
Overall, DaGoal is designed to provide users with a practical and engaging way
to manage their screen time while building healthier daily habits. By combining
real-world activities, flexible task options, progress tracking, rewards, and
screen time control, the application gives users more than just a way to monitor
device usage. It encourages them to take action, stay consistent, and make better
use of their time through meaningful activities outside the screen.

Scope
The study focuses on the design, development, and initial evaluation of DaGoal,
a gamified daily activity planner designed to help reduce excessive screen time

influence user consistency, the study remains committed to evaluating how
gamified rewards and personalized task generation can effectively transition

Forest relies on restricting device use for a set period, the proposed system
differs by encouraging active participation in structured, real-world tasks.

applications and temporarily restricts or interrupts access through overlay or
redirection to them while a timer-based quest is active, combining restriction

implemented its major planned functions as a gamified daily activity planner.
The application was able to generate personalized quests, verify task

and provide screen time control features. The functional testing results also
showed that the tested features operated according to their expected behavior.

progress tracking, and screen time control. However, since the evaluation lasted
only three days and relied mainly on user feedback, the results should be
considered an initial evaluation rather than proof of long-term screen time
reduction.

measure changes in screen time habits. Future versions may also expand the
available quests, rewards, and avatar customization options and further improve
app-blocking, step tracking, data synchronization, performance, and overall
