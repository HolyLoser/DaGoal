Delete your DaGoal account

In the app, open Settings, choose Account, then Delete Account. Use the email option to request deletion.

You can also email shaunocana2004@gmail.com and request deletion of your DaGoal account. Send the request from the email address associated with the account. Never send your password. The development team may need to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

After verifying the request, the development team deletes the Firebase login and associated cloud data. To remove local data from a device, clear DaGoal storage in Android Settings. Android device backups and provider operational records may have separate retention settings.

Guest users can remove local progress by clearing DaGoal storage in Android Settings. Read the privacy policy for details about information used by the app.
