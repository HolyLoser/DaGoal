Delete your DaGoal account

In the app, open Settings, choose Account, then Delete Account. Follow the confirmation and sign-in prompts. An internet connection is needed to complete Firebase account and cloud-profile deletion.

If you cannot access the app, email shaunocana2004@gmail.com and request deletion of your DaGoal account. Include the account email address, but never send your password. The development team may need to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Successful in-app deletion clears the Firebase user profile, authentication account, active local account data, and the account-specific local backup. Android device backups and provider operational records may have separate retention settings.

Guest users can remove local progress by clearing DaGoal storage in Android Settings. Read the privacy policy for details about information used by the app.
