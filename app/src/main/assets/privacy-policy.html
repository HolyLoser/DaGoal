DaGoal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

DaGoal is a capstone daily activity planner developed for students and young adults. It uses quests, virtual rewards, and avatar customization to support healthier daily routines. The capstone documentation identifies the project with STI College Pasay-EDSA; this policy describes the DaGoal application and its development team, not an institutional privacy policy.

Contact the DaGoal development team at shaunocana2004@gmail.com for privacy questions, access/correction requests, or help deleting your account.

Information the app uses

  • Account information: If you register, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password. DaGoal stores an account identifier and email to connect your saved profile to your account. The app does not store your password in plaintext in its local preferences or database.
  • Profile and setup choices: Your nickname, age-group information, interests, activity preferences, and questionnaire choices help personalize quests. Some choices are converted into preference settings rather than retained as the original answer.
  • Game and activity progress: Quests, recurring schedules, completion progress, rewards, virtual Gold, XP, level, streak dates, achievements, inventory, equipped cosmetics, and avatar choices are stored to operate the app.
  • Step information: With activity-recognition permission, supported device sensors provide step counts to verify walking quests. Step-quest progress may be included in saved quest backups; the app does not need your location for step tracking.
  • App-selection and usage information: With Usage Access and overlay permissions, DaGoal identifies when selected distracting apps are opened and displays an interruption during an active avoidance quest. Selected app identifiers can be stored with custom quests and their backups. DaGoal does not read messages, passwords, or the contents of other apps.
  • Support and operational information: If you choose to send feedback, your email app prepares a message that includes app version, device model, and Android version. You choose whether to send it. Online shop rotation and chest-claim event records may also be sent to Firebase to support those features.

Why this information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, personalize and verify quests, calculate rewards, display progress, restore saved schedules and inventory, operate selected screen-time controls, and respond to support requests. The current app has no advertising SDK or real-money purchase system. In-app Gold is virtual currency.

Local storage and cloud services

Guest play and the core quest features use local device storage. Registered accounts use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for online authentication and supported profile, avatar, inventory, and recurring-schedule backups. Data sent to these services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Google processes service data under its own terms and privacy documentation: Firebase privacy and security information and Google Privacy Policy.

Android may also back up app data or transfer it between devices according to your device and Google-account backup settings. This is separate from DaGoal's own Firebase synchronization.

Permissions and your choices

  • Activity recognition: verifies supported step quests.
  • Usage Access and display over other apps: support selected-app avoidance during active quests.
  • Notifications and foreground services: show reminders and the status of ongoing tracking or avoidance.
  • Battery-optimization exemption: is requested to improve reliability of selected background features.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android Settings. Features depending on a revoked permission may stop working. The current app does not declare an Accessibility Service.

Retention, logout, and deletion

Local progress is retained while you use the app. Logging out clears the active local account state, while an account-specific local backup may remain so unsynced recurring quests and progress can be restored after you authenticate as the same account. A different account cannot load that backup through the app's login flow.

To request deletion, open Settings > Account > Delete Account. If in-app deletion is unavailable, use the support email above. The app may ask you to sign in again. An internet connection is needed to complete deletion of the Firebase account and profile. Successful account deletion also clears the active local account data and that account's local backup. You can also email the contact above to request help with account or data deletion.

You can remove guest data by clearing DaGoal's storage in Android Settings. Removing the app clears its active local installation; this version excludes app data from Android automatic backup and device transfer. Copies made by earlier versions may remain according to your system settings. Provider backups and service records may have separate retention periods controlled by the service provider. No fixed deletion period is promised for copies outside the app's direct control.

Students and younger users

The documented research audience is students and young adults; the evaluation sample was aged 19 to 26. That sample is not a minimum-age rule. If you are below the age at which you may independently consent to data processing where you live, involve a parent or guardian before creating an account. Parents and guardians can contact the development team about a younger user's data or deletion.

Security and changes

The app uses account authentication, account-specific backup ownership checks, and encrypted connections. No system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may change when the app's data practices change; the updated date and policy will be available in the app.
